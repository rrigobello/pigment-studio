How accurate is this?

This prototype uses generic paint swatches and a three-channel Kubelka–Munk-inspired approximation, then minimizes distance in Oklab. It does not use measured pigment spectra, tinting strength, or brand calibration. The displayed match describes the model only; physical paint may differ substantially.

The search is deterministic: fixed paint order, a fixed 5% proportion grid, and no randomness. Percentages describe model proportions, not a verified weight or volume formula. Use them as approximate equal-size parts for a small test. Dry fully and compare under the same lighting.
